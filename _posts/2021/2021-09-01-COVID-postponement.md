## Postponing due to COVID

With the ongoing public health situation and how things were tracking across Australia, we made the call to **postpone** our **2021** event.

We rescheduled **BSides Brisbane** to align with our next planned date: **Saturday 16 July 2022**. Thanks for sticking with us while we sorted it out.
