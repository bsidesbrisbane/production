## This year's sponsors
The board is empty. We do not have sponsors locked in yet, and a conference like this does not run on atmosphere alone. BSides Brisbane stays technical and stays cheap at the door because organisations put money, coffee, hardware, and a venue behind the Saturday. If that is you, the [sponsorship page](/sponsorship/) has the prospectus and a way to get in touch.

Tiers run Platinum, Diamond, Gold, Silver, and Bronze. We also look for people to cover the electronic badge, the venue, coffee for the crew, and prizes for the CTF. Local Queensland outfits still get a look in through the small-business upgrade on the sponsorship page. Same room. Same underground brief. Your name on the wall instead of a blank frame.
